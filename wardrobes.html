Openable Wardrobes Design

Classic openable wardrobes combine practical storage with elegant finishes to bring organization and lasting style to any bedroom.

  • Panoramic 180-Degree Access: Wide-opening soft-close hinges allow all doors to open at once for an instant view of all clothes.

Category 04 • Folding
Bi-Fold Engineering • Hybrid Performance

Folding Wardrobes Design

Combining the best of sliding and hinged systems, folding wardrobes open completely to give you full visibility and access to your entire closet while minimizing door swing clearance.

  • Concertina Bi-Fold Panels: Doors fold smoothly against the outer wardrobe jambs with half the swing radius of traditional hinged doors.
  • Total Internal Exposure: Unlike 2-door sliding wardrobes where 50% is always blocked, folding doors expose 100% of the interior simultaneously.
  • German WingLine Track System: Smooth-gliding top and bottom guidance hardware designed for effortless one-touch opening.
