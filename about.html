Manufacturing Hub

Inside Our 15,000+ Sq.Ft Bhagabanpur Factory

Located at Plot #12, Bhagabanpur Industrial Estate, Patrapada, Bhubaneswar. All cabinetry is pre-machined on computer-controlled German machinery for zero-defect precision.

German CNC Beam Saws

Automated multi-blade panel saws cut calibrated plywood boards with ±0.1mm micro-tolerance, guaranteeing razor-sharp 90-degree corners with zero chipping or tear-out.

Zero-Joint Edge Banding

High-temperature EVA and PUR hot-melt edge banders apply 2mm thick PVC edge strips seamlessly, sealing wood cores completely against moisture, steam, and humidity.

Multi-Spindle Boring

Precision CNC multi-head boring machines drill minifix cam, wooden dowel, and hinge pocket holes at exact factory coordinates, ensuring zero carpenter hammering on site.

Dust-Free PU Spray Booth

Pressurized, climate-controlled spray painting chamber where Italian polyurethane lacquers are applied in multiple coats for mirror-smooth automotive-grade luster.

Full Factory Pre-Assembly

Every kitchen and wardrobe carcass is dry-assembled and tested for drawer alignment at our factory prior to flat-pack shipment, eliminating on-site installation surprises.

45-Day Handover Guarantee

Strict commitment to on-time handover. Because our factory operates 6 days a week with automated machinery, we honor our move-in schedule without delay excuses.
