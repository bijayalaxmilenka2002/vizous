Showroom & Consultation FAQs

Helpful information before visiting our Laxmisagar showroom or scheduling a site measurement.

Do I need an appointment to visit the Laxmisagar showroom? +
Walk-ins are always welcome from Monday through Saturday, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. However, booking an appointment in advance ensures a senior interior architect is dedicated exclusively to reviewing your floorplan.
Is the initial site measurement and 3D design session free? +
Yes, 100% free with zero obligation. Our architect will visit your property in Bhubaneswar or Cuttack with laser measurement tools, inspect plumbing and electrical points, and present a photorealistic 3D layout.
Can I visit the Bhagabanpur factory to inspect my cabinetry during fabrication? +
Yes! We maintain an open-door policy. You are invited to visit Plot #12, Bhagabanpur Industrial Estate while your kitchen or wardrobes are being cut and edge-banded to verify plywood stamps and German hardware.
