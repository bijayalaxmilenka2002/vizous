Why Vizous In-House Factory Direct Is Better Than Traditional Shops

Unlike local contractors who cut raw boards on your floor or retail shops that outsource to third-party carpentry sheds, Vizous crafts every modular piece in our own 15,000+ sq.ft industrial plant in Bhubaneswar.

In-House Manufacturing Plant Plot #12, Bhagabanpur Industrial Estate, Patrapada, Bhubaneswar-751019

At Vizous, manufacturing isn't outsourced—it is our core engineering backbone. Our dedicated facility is equipped with automated German Homag CNC beam saws, computer-guided multi-spindle drilling lines, and high-pressure zero-joint edge banders. Every modular kitchen carcass and luxury wardrobe module is precision-cut to a 0.1mm industrial tolerance using authentic, certified 100% IS:710 Marine Grade BWP Plywood. This means zero on-site carpenter mess in your living room, zero toxic fumes, and true factory-direct wholesale pricing with no middleman markups.

Market Risk Factors

Traditional Shops & Local Carpenters

Outsourced workshops & manual site carpentry
  • Inflated Middleman Markups (+25% to 40%)

    Multiple layers of commission agents, retailer margins, and contractor cuts add steep costs without adding any quality.

  • Manual Hand-Saw Cuts & Chipped Laminates

    Hand-held circular saws produce uneven edges, chipped laminate corners, sagging drawers, and misaligned hinge holes.

  • Uncertified Commercial MR Ply / Particle Board

    Often uses inferior commercial ply that absorbs dampness, bends under load, and attracts termite hollows within 12 to 24 months.

  • Manual Fevicol Edge Tape (Peels Within Months)

    Manual cold adhesives fail against kitchen steam and moisture, causing edge strips to detach and expose the core wood.

  • Months of Toxic Sawdust, Noise & Delays

    60 to 90 days of loud hammering, dust on walls, toxic glue odors, and unpredictable carpenter absconding schedules.

  • Zero Post-Installation Accountability

    Verbal promises evaporate after final settlement; no formal repair SLA, no replacement guarantees, and zero warranty backing.
