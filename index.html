Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our materials, warranties, factory visits, and handover guarantees.

What materials and plywood grades does Vizous use? +
We exclusively use 100% IS:710 Boiling Water Proof (BWP) Marine Grade Plywood for wet zones (kitchens and vanities) and IS:303 BWR / Commercial ply for dry bedroom zones. Surface finishes include German acrylics, Italian PU lacquers, and scratch-resistant decorative laminates from CenturyPly, Greenpanel, and Merino.
Can I visit your factory before placing an order? +
Yes, absolutely! We encourage all homeowners and architects to visit our 15,000+ sq.ft manufacturing facility at Plot #12, Bhagabanpur Industrial Estate, Patrapada, Bhubaneswar. You can inspect the German Homag CNC beam saws, automated edge-banding lines, and board raw materials firsthand.
What does the 10-Year Warranty cover? +
Our 10-year warranty provides replacement coverage against termite infestation, borer attacks, and delamination of our IS:710 marine plywood carcasses, as well as manufacturer replacement on all Hettich and Hafele soft-close hinges, tandem runners, and hydraulic lift mechanisms.
How does the 45-day handover guarantee work? +
Once you approve the 3D drawings and sign off on materials, your clock begins. Because all components are pre-machined at our Bhagabanpur plant, on-site installation takes only 5 to 7 days, ensuring total handover within 45 working days.
